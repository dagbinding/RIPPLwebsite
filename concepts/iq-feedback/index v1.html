What RIPPLiQ can say.

Any overlay above can carry any of these. The rule that keeps it watch-sized: one score, one cause, one cue. Everything here comes from the board sensor, the pose model, or both.

from the pose model

The body

  • Knee bend, front and backCompression through each turn; the clearest "stand tall, fall" signal in this ride.
  • Hips over feetWeight forward or back of centre at the moment it went wrong.
  • Shoulders leading the turnUpper-body rotation ahead of the board, or late.
  • Lead arm and eyesPointing where you want to go, or dropping.
from RIPPLsense

The board

  • Rail angleHow far you lean into each turn (45° max on this wave).
  • SpeedGained or bled through each turn (14 to 26 km/h here).
  • Turn count and timingTakeoff, bottom turns, top turns, where it ended.
  • Ride heightUp and down the face, so you see where on the wave each turn happened.
from your model

The verdict

  • Wave scoreOne number, comparable wave to wave.
  • Why it endedFall detected, and the most likely cause.
  • You vs. youThis turn against your best one, today or all season.
  • One cue for the next setTimed to the watch's next-set countdown.

Putting it on the film.

  1. Film is the Higgsfield clip (1280 × 720, 5 s). The watch screen is tracked frame by frame, so the brackets, the tethers and the D card stay on it as the hand bobs. The overlay runs on the film's clock and the film holds its last frame while the overlay finishes.
  2. Start at is when the overlay begins, in film seconds. 1.3 s catches the watch as it comes up, so the brackets lock on while it settles.
  3. Photo and Your clip aren't tracked: use Move watch to drag the brackets onto the watch screen and set its size. It's remembered on this browser.
  4. To composite in an editor: Green screen (it keeps the film's timing and tracking), then Clean (or H) and Fullscreen, and screen-record at 1920 × 1080. Key out the green and lay it over the clip from frame 0; extend the clip's last frame to cover the hold.
  5. The ride is real: session 2272, the rider rebuilt by the pose model, standing on RIPPL's board CAD, tilted by the board's own IMU. Knee angles, rail and speed are measured; the wave score, wave number and next-set time are placeholders.
