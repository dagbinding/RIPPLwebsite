FAQ. Frequently asked questions

Everything surfers ask us before they join the lineup. Missing one? Ask us on Instagram.

What is RIPPL?

RIPPL is a tiny sensor for the tail of your surfboard, plus an app that turns every ride into coaching, stats and leaderboards. RIPPLsense records the ride, RIPPLintelligence explains it, and RIPPLsocial lets you share it with your crew.

How does it work?

Snap RIPPLsense onto your tail and surf — no phone in the water. It records every movement of your board, then syncs to the RIPPL app once you're back on the sand, where every wave is broken down and scored.

Wear a paired watch and you'll get feedback live, the moment you kick out.

How will RIPPL improve my surfing?

RIPPL shows you the why behind every wave. You get AI coaching on each ride — like opening your shoulders earlier on the bottom turn — training plans that adapt to you and your break, and a clear read on how this session compares with your last.

With a watch on, you can adjust before the next wave, not next session.

Is RIPPL for beginners?

Yes — RIPPL is for every level. Coaching adapts to where you are: if you're learning, it shows you what to work on next; if you've surfed for years, it finds the details you can't feel.

Will it fit my board?

RIPPLsense fits any board — shortboard, longboard or softtop. Stick a mount on each of your boards; the sensor snaps in and out in seconds, so you can change boards mid-session and get straight back in the lineup.

Is it waterproof?

Yes. RIPPLsense is rated IP68 and built from marine-grade materials, made to live in saltwater, sand and sun.

How long does the battery last?

Multiple sessions on a single charge — days of surfing between charges. Charge it, attach it before you paddle out, then forget about it.

What does it actually measure?

A 9-axis IMU and centimeter-precise GNSS read every movement of your board. From that we measure each wave you ride, wave height, turn power, paddle outs and paddle efficiency — then score every wave and every session.

How is that different from a surf watch app?

A watch sits on your wrist, so it mostly knows where you went. RIPPLsense sits on the board — the thing you're actually steering — so it reads how you rode each wave, not just that you caught it.

Does it work at any beach, or just wave pools?

Both. RIPPL works at any break — beach, reef or point — and in wave pools too.

Can I share my sessions to social media?

RIPPL is built around sharing with your crew and competing on the leaderboard at your local break. Posting sessions straight to Instagram and TikTok is on our roadmap.

When will RIPPL be available?

We're starting our tester program soon. Join the waitlist to find out first.

How much will it cost?

We haven't announced pricing yet. If you're picked as a tester, your RIPPLsense is free.

How does the tester program work?

Join the waitlist and we hand-pick our first riders. Testers get a free RIPPLsense, shipped worldwide, first access to every feature, and a direct line to the founders.

Every surfer you refer moves you both up the list. Five accepted referrals guarantees your sensor in Drop 2; ten gets you and your whole crew into Drop 1.
